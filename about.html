Our story

A neighbourhood bakery with a handmade approach

North Star Bakery began as a small neighbourhood baking project built around the idea that good bread and sweet treats can bring people together. Today, the bakery continues that handmade approach while welcoming regular customers, commuters, families, and local event planners.

Our sourcing philosophy

We aim to choose dependable ingredients from nearby suppliers whenever practical. We focus on straightforward recipes, careful preparation, and baking in small batches so customers can enjoy fresh products throughout the day.

Meet the bakery team

The Baker

Leads daily bread and pastry production and develops seasonal specials.

The Front Counter Team

Helps customers choose products, answers questions, and prepares pre-orders for pickup.

The Celebration Baker

Coordinates cakes and larger requests for family gatherings and community events.

Behind the scenes

From mixing dough to serving customers, our daily process is built around small batches and careful preparation.

1. Prepare

Mixing and shaping

Ingredients are measured, dough is mixed, and breads and pastries are shaped by hand in small batches.

2. Bake

Fresh from the ovens

Each batch is baked carefully so the bakery can offer fresh breads, flaky pastries, and treats throughout the day.

3. Share

Ready for the counter

Finished products are prepared for the display counter or set aside for customers who have pre-ordered.
